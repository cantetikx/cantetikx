### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Linux System Administrator** focused on system administration and infrastructure automation. I've built hands-on experience with high-performance computing (HPC) clusters, virtualization technologies, and distributed file systems — currently at Eduline Bilişim, where I'm responsible for the installation, management, and performance optimization of Linux-based HPC and enterprise AI infrastructure.

Throughout my career, I've worked on operating **KVM/Proxmox** virtualization platforms on top of **BeeGFS** distributed storage, deploying and managing production **Kubernetes** clusters, and building end-to-end observability stacks with **Grafana + Prometheus + XDMoD**. On the İŞKUR GPU cluster project, I worked with **AWS S3** buckets for cloud storage, and I'm now actively growing my skills in **AWS Cloud**, **Infrastructure as Code (IaC)**, and **GitOps**.

My goal is to bridge the gap between development and operations teams by making systems more reliable, scalable, and automated. Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) and Cloud Native technologies are my ongoing learning focus.

- 🖥️ Currently: **Linux System Administrator** @ Eduline Bilişim (Istanbul)
- 🎓 Chemical Engineering @ Hacettepe University + Computer Programming @ Istanbul University (AUZEF)
- ☁️ Learning: **AWS Cloud**, **Infrastructure as Code**, **GitOps**, **SRE**
- 📍 Based in Ankara, Türkiye | 🌐 EN (C1) · DE (A2)
- 🤝 Open to new opportunities and collaborations — feel free to reach out!
